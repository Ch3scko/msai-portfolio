Current Projects

New Horizons

New Horizons was my capstone project at Spartanburg Methodist College, about the stress and mental health problems that international students face. I led it from start to finish: I designed and ran a survey, studied the answers, held peer focus groups, and made TikTok videos with practical advice for students. It showed that homesickness, adjusting to a new culture and a lack of peer support were the main problems, gave the college leadership recommendations, and was a key part of my graduation.

Keep One Spartanburg Beautiful

Keep One Spartanburg Beautiful is a community organization in Spartanburg, South Carolina, that runs environmental education and volunteer programs. From August to December 2024, I worked with them as a student consultant and reviewed how their community and education programs were run, to find where things were getting stuck. I gave them recommendations for reaching more volunteers and getting more people in the community involved.

AU Flyer Skill

A Claude skill that asks for an event's details and then makes an Atlantis University flyer in the right school colour. I did not write or change this skill: our class built it, and I used it, answering its questions about the event so it could build the flyer. It showed me how a few clear answers can turn into a finished, print-ready flyer.
